1. About RepChamp

RepChamp is a fitness competition app that uses your phone camera and on-device AI to count push-ups and squats. The Service includes:

  • Solo training and workout tracking
  • Real-time multiplayer duels and matchmaking
  • AI opponents, leaderboards, badges, and progression
  • Friends, chat, and social features

2. Eligibility

You must be at least 16 years old to use RepChamp.

3. Account Deletion

You may delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete Account or by contacting support at repchampapp@gmail.com.

4. Health and Safety Disclaimer

RepChamp is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Consult a physician before starting any exercise program. Exercise carries inherent risks of injury.

5. Camera and Privacy

RepChamp requires camera access to count reps. Video is processed entirely on your device and is never uploaded or recorded on our servers.
