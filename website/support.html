Account & Data Deletion Request

You can permanently delete your Fitness Duel: RepChamp account and everything stored with it. Deletion removes your profile and profile picture, your leaderboard entry, your matchmaking ticket, your friend and blocked lists, your push notification token, and any shared couple record. Pending duel invitations are cancelled and any live match you are in is forfeited so your opponent is not left waiting.

Workout history stored only on your own phone is removed when you uninstall the app. Anonymous, non-identifying analytics that cannot be traced back to you are not deleted, because there is nothing in them to link to your account.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion

1. Open Fitness Duel: RepChamp on your device.
2. Go to Profile > the settings gear > Your Data.
3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This runs immediately rather than queuing a request. You can also tap Export my data on the same screen to take a copy first.

Deleting your data without closing your account

Fitness Duel: RepChamp does not keep a separate store of data you can clear while keeping the account — your data is your account. Deleting the account, by either method here, deletes all of it. There is no partial option to choose between.

If you only want a copy of what is held, tap Export my data under Profile > settings gear > Your Data. That hands you a JSON file and changes nothing.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you can no longer sign in, email arafathossain455@gmail.com from the address on your account with "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line, and include your username so the account can be identified.

Requests are answered within 30 days and usually much sooner. Deleting in the app is immediate, so use that if you still have access.
