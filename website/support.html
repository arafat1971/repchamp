Account & Data Deletion Request

If you wish to permanently delete your RepChamp account and all associated cloud data (authentication profile, social duel stats, and leaderboard entries), you can do so through any of the methods below:

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion

1. Open the RepChamp App on your device.
2. Navigate to Settings > Account.
3. Tap Delete Account & Data and confirm.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

Fill out the support contact form below or send an email directly to support@peachtraders.xyz with the email address associated with your account. Include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line.

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. All remote authentication records, profile entries, and workout syncing data will be permanently removed.
